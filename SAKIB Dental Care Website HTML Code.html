Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Essential information regarding Sakib Dental Care's branch hours, Dental X-Ray, and treatments

What are the visiting hours for the Nowhata and City branches?

Our Nowhata Branch (East of Akbari Mosque, Nowhata Bazar) is open from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Closed on Fridays). Our City Branch (Kasaikhana More, Jinnahnagar, Sopura, Rajshahi) is open every evening from 7:00 PM to 11:00 PM.

Do you perform Dental X-Rays (Imaging) inside the clinic?

Yes! Dental X-Rays (Imaging) are performed here with utmost care and precision. Patients do not need to travel elsewhere for root canal or wisdom tooth radiographs.

Can broken or discolored front teeth be restored naturally?

Absolutely. We provide Laser Tooth-Color Matching Aesthetic Fillings as well as high-strength Porcelain & Zirconia Crowns and Bridges to restore broken or missing teeth back to their natural appearance.

How can I book a serial or contact the clinic directly?

You can book online using our instant form above, message us on WhatsApp at 01733-054717, or call our dedicated serial hotline at 01688-434676.
